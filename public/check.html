Update · 18 Oct 2026

From joetom, Qubic Core team

What happened. Some wallet seeds were generated with insufficient randomness. Seeds created on these combinations are affected:

  • iPhone / iPad, any browser: seeds created before 2026-10-18
  • MacBook / iMac with Safari: seeds created before 2026-10-18
  • Qubic iOS app: seeds created before July 2025

The funds are contained. The network is paused: computors coordinated a protective halt at tick 85,371,949, so the drained funds cannot be moved or sold. Exchanges have suspended Qubic deposits and withdrawals. Seed generation is fixed in version 4.26.4.

Next. Any resolution is a decision for the computor quorum. A community poll will give them a recommendation. A full technical report will follow once recovery is settled.

From Kimz, community board

12.9% of the funds (379.04B QU) went to a single MEXC deposit address; MEXC froze that account at ~01:52 UTC. The other 87.1% (2.56T QU) is still on 8 attacker wallets on the Qubic network and has not moved since 01:26 UTC. A full report was sent to MEXC and other exchanges listing QU were notified.

Never paste your seed or private key here, or anywhere else. Only your public address.

What to do

  1. No need to rush. There is nothing to do on-chain right now: the network is stopped for everyone.
  2. If your address was drained, file a police / cybercrime report in your country. Include your wallet ID, the amount lost and the transaction link: this page shows all three when you check your address.
  3. Treat your seed as weak if it was created with one of the combinations above, even if your address is not on the list. When the network is back, follow the official instructions.
  4. Follow only official Qubic channels for the outcome of the computor vote.
Beware of scammers. Nobody from the Qubic team will ever DM you first, ask for your seed, or offer “recovery services” for a fee. Report such messages to the mods.
