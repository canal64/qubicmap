QUBIC MERGE

Road to Aigarth

Drop spheres in the jar. Two identical spheres merge into the next tier.

  • Aim by moving your finger or mouse, release to drop.
  • Merges in a row make a combo and multiply your points.
  • Merges charge the ⚡ Quorum Pulse: it destroys the 3 smallest spheres and shakes the jar.
  • Stay under the red line: above it for 2.5 s and it's over.
