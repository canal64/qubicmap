QUBIC BUBBLE

Road to Aigarth

Shoot bubbles up at the ceiling. Hit 3 or more of the same kind to pop them.

  • Aim with your finger or mouse, release to shoot. Bounce off the walls for tricky shots.
  • Bubbles left hanging with nothing above them fall for a big bonus.
  • Pops in a row make a combo. The ceiling comes down one row every 8 shots (a little sooner on higher levels). You have 4 seconds per shot.
  • Pop a group next to a Qubic bubble to set it off: it blasts everything around it. Make it fall for a bonus too.
  • Lucky $CFB coin in your cannon? It takes the colour of what it hits, pops even a pair, and pumps the points x2.
  • Drop 10 or more bubbles in one shot to earn the GOD bubble (once per board): wherever it lands, every bubble of that colour on the board is wiped out.
  • Pops charge the ⚡ Quorum Pulse: it blasts the lowest row.
  • Every level has a goal: clear the board, rescue the caged Computors, reach the Tick at the top, or vote a Quorum before your shots run out.
  • New blocks show up as you climb: ice, locked SCs, burn blocks and bombs. Reach each goal to climb from Trit all the way to Aigarth.
