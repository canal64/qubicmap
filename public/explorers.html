Common questions

How do I check a Qubic transaction?

Paste the transaction ID or the address into Qubic Explorer. Qubic Report also shows the transactions of each tick as they happen.

What is a tick?

A tick is one step of the Qubic network, during which the computors agree on a set of transactions. An epoch groups the ticks of one week.
